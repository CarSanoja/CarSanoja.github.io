I train language models against the clock.

I’m Carlos Sanoja, an ML research engineer. In 2026 I placed 3rd in Phase 1 of the AWS Trainium Frontier Competition, pretraining a model from scratch on one Trainium2 chip in 30 minutes, and I’m now in its 10-team final phase.

I also build agent systems that stop and hand control back to a person when the evidence runs out, and I write up what did not work as carefully as what did. I founded Quanta Labs, which builds AI tools for schools in Latin America, and I teach at Universidad Monteávila. Before language models I worked on robotics: SLAM, 3D perception and reinforcement learning for manipulators.

Aug to Oct 2026 3rd place, Phase 1

Pretraining against the clock on one Trainium2 chip

AWS Trainium Frontier Competition. Train a language model from scratch in 30 minutes of wall-clock on a single Trainium2 chip, scored on held-out bits per byte. I treated it as a measurement problem: profile first, change one thing, climb a validation ladder before paying for a full run, and pre-register the rule that decides.

Official val bpb 0.947182, down from 1.1728 over 16 submissions
