About

I’m an ML research engineer based in Spain. I work on language-model pretraining under fixed compute, on agent systems that keep a person in charge of the decisions that matter, and on Kaggle and ARC Prize competitions. I founded Quanta Labs and I teach at Universidad Monteávila.

What I do now

Founder, Quanta Labs (2025 to present)

Quanta builds AI tools for K-12 schools in Latin America: a multi-tenant learning platform, AI-assisted grading in which the teacher keeps the final word, and offline dictation for teachers. I build it end to end: a FastAPI and Celery backend on PostgreSQL with pgvector, a TypeScript monorepo for the web portals, an Expo mobile app, and the AWS infrastructure in Terraform.

How I work

  • State the hypothesis before the measurement. Every run in my ledgers has its prediction written down first, so a result can contradict me.
  • Climb a ladder before paying for the full run. CPU check, one core, a smoke run, a short run, then the expensive one. Most bad ideas die on the cheap rungs.
  • Know the noise floor. I measure seed spread and pair noise before believing a difference, and I say which reference a number is measured against.
  • Let a reviewer try to break it. Claims go through an adversarial review before anything is built on top of them.
  • Publish the failures. Every write-up on this site has a section on what did not work, with numbers.
